# War

War is a simple card game for 2 players requiring no skill — luck alone determines the winner.

## Setup
The deck is shuffled and dealt evenly: 26 cards face-down to each player.

## Gameplay
Each player simultaneously flips their top card face-up. The player with the higher card wins both cards and places them at the bottom of their pile. Cards rank 2 (low) through Ace (high). Suits do not matter.

## War
When both players flip the same rank, **war** is declared:
1. Each player places 3 cards face-down
2. Then flips 1 card face-up
3. Higher face-up card wins all cards on the table
4. If tied again, war repeats

## Winning
The player who collects all 52 cards wins. A player who runs out of cards mid-war loses.
