# High Card

The simplest game in the deck. Two players, one card each, higher card wins.

## Players

2 players — you and one opponent.

## The Deck

A standard 52-card deck.

## How to Play

1. Tap to flip. Both players turn over the top card of the deck at the same time.
2. Compare the two cards. The higher rank wins the round and collects both cards.
3. Tap to continue, and play the next round.

Suits do not matter — only rank.

## Card Ranking

From lowest to highest:

**2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace**

Aces are always high.

## Ties

When both cards are the same rank, the round is a tie. By default each player takes
their own card back and play continues.

## Winning

Play continues until the deck runs out. The player who has collected the most cards
wins.

## House Rules

- **Ties Split the Pot** — instead of each player taking their card back, a tied round
  leaves both cards in the pot for the winner of the next round to claim.
