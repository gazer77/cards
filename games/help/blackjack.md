# Blackjack

Blackjack (also called 21) is played against the dealer. The goal is to have a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

## Card Values
- Number cards (2–10): face value
- Jack, Queen, King: 10
- Ace: 1 or 11 (whichever benefits the hand)

## Gameplay
Each player and the dealer receive 2 cards. Player cards are face-up; the dealer shows one card and keeps one face-down.

**Player options:**
- **Hit** — Take another card
- **Stand** — Keep current hand
- **Double Down** — Double your bet, take exactly one more card
- **Split** — If dealt a pair, split into two separate hands

## Dealer Rules
After all players act, the dealer reveals their hidden card. The dealer must hit on 16 or below and stand on 17 or above.

## Outcomes
- **Bust** (over 21): Lose immediately
- **Blackjack** (Ace + 10-value card): Pays 3:2
- **Closer to 21 than dealer**: Win even money
- **Tie (push)**: Bet returned
