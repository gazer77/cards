# Poker with Wilds

Poker with Wilds follows Texas Hold'em rules with one addition: Deuces (2s) are wild cards and can substitute for any card to complete a hand.

## Wild Cards
A wild 2 can represent any card you choose — any rank and any suit. Use it to complete a straight, flush, or any other combination.

## Hand Rankings
Wild cards can create a hand rank that would otherwise be impossible. When two players hold the same hand rank, the player using fewer wild cards wins. If still tied, standard kicker rules apply.

**Five of a Kind** is possible with wilds and ranks above a Royal Flush.

## Everything Else
All other rules — blinds, betting rounds (Pre-flop, Flop, Turn, River), betting options (Check, Bet, Raise, Call, Fold), and showdown — are identical to Texas Hold'em.

See the **Texas Hold'em** help page for full gameplay details.
