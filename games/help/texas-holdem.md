# Texas Hold'em

Texas Hold'em is the most popular poker variant. Each player receives 2 private cards and shares 5 community cards to make the best 5-card hand.

## Setup
One player is the dealer (marked by the dealer button). The two players to the left post the small blind and big blind (forced bets).

## Betting Rounds
1. **Pre-flop:** Deal 2 hole cards to each player. Betting starts left of the big blind.
2. **Flop:** Deal 3 community cards face-up. Betting starts left of the dealer.
3. **Turn:** Deal 1 more community card. Another betting round.
4. **River:** Deal the final community card. Final betting round.

## Betting Options
- **Check** — Pass the action (only if no bet has been made)
- **Bet / Raise** — Place or increase a bet
- **Call** — Match the current bet
- **Fold** — Discard your hand and forfeit the round

## Showdown
If 2+ players remain after the river, reveal hands. The player with the best 5-card combination using any combination of their 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards wins the pot.

## Hand Rankings (high to low)
Royal Flush, Straight Flush, Four of a Kind, Full House, Flush, Straight, Three of a Kind, Two Pair, One Pair, High Card.

## Winning
Win chips by having the best hand at showdown or by making all opponents fold. Last player with chips wins the game.
