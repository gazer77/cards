# Spades

Spades is a trick-taking game for 4 players in 2 partnerships. Spades are always trump.

## Setup
Partners sit across from each other. Deal all 52 cards (13 each).

## Bidding
Before play, each player bids the number of tricks they expect to win. The partnership's bids are combined into a team bid. Bidding "Nil" means you bid zero tricks — risky but rewarding.

## Gameplay
The player to the dealer's left leads first. Players must follow suit if able. If unable to follow suit, play any card including a spade. The highest card of the led suit wins unless a spade is played — the highest spade wins.

Spades may not be led until they have been "broken" (a spade has been played on a previous trick) unless a player has only spades remaining.

## Scoring
- **Made bid:** 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 point per overtrick (bag)
- **Failed bid:** Minus 10 points per trick bid
- **Nil bid made:** +100 points; **Nil bid failed:** −100 points
- **Bags:** Every 10 accumulated overtricks costs 100 points

## Winning
First partnership to reach 500 points wins. A partnership at −200 or below may be eliminated by house rule.
