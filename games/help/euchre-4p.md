# Euchre (4-Player)

Euchre is a trick-taking game for 4 players in 2 partnerships using a 24-card deck (9s through Aces).

## Trump and the Bowers
The suit chosen as trump is special:
- **Right Bower:** Jack of trump suit — highest card
- **Left Bower:** Jack of the same-color suit — second highest
- Trump rank (high to low): Right Bower, Left Bower, A, K, Q, 10, 9

## Dealing
Deal 5 cards to each player in batches of 2 and 3. Flip the top remaining card face-up to propose trump.

## Calling Trump
Going clockwise from the dealer's left:
- **Round 1:** Each player may order up (accept the flipped card's suit as trump) or pass. If ordered up, the dealer takes the card and discards one.
- **Round 2:** If all pass in round 1, the flipped card is turned down and each player may name any other suit as trump or pass. If all pass again, the hand is redealt.

The player or partnership that calls trump must win at least 3 of 5 tricks.

## Gameplay
Player to dealer's left leads. Players must follow suit if able. Highest trump wins if any trump is played; otherwise highest card of led suit wins. Winner of each trick leads the next.

## Going Alone
The player who calls trump may go alone (partner sits out) for a bonus if successful.

## Scoring
- **Called trump, won 3–4 tricks:** 1 point
- **Called trump, won all 5 (march):** 2 points
- **Went alone, won all 5:** 4 points
- **Euchred (called trump, won fewer than 3):** Opponents score 2 points

## Winning
First partnership to reach 10 points wins.
