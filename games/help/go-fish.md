# Go Fish

Go Fish is a card game for 2–6 players. The goal is to collect the most **books** — sets of 4 matching cards.

## Setup
With 2–3 players, deal 7 cards each. With 4+ players, deal 5 cards each. Remaining cards form the draw pile (the ocean).

## Gameplay
On your turn, ask any other player for a specific rank. You must hold at least one card of that rank yourself.

- **They have it:** They hand over all cards of that rank. Take another turn.
- **They do not:** They say "Go Fish!" You draw the top card from the ocean. If you drew the rank you asked for, show it and take another turn.

## Books
When you collect all 4 cards of a rank, place the book face-up in front of you.

## Winning
Play until all cards are matched into books. The player with the most books wins.
