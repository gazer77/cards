# Hand and Foot

Hand and Foot is a canasta-style game for 2–6 players (best with 4 in partnerships). Each player is dealt two sets of cards: a "hand" and a "foot."

## Setup
Use 5 standard decks plus jokers (270 cards total). Deal 11 cards face-down for the hand and 11 cards face-down for the foot to each player. Players pick up their hand first; the foot is set aside until the hand is exhausted.

## Card Values
- Joker: 50 points
- Ace, 2 (wild): 20 points
- 8–King: 10 points
- 4–7: 5 points
- 3 (red): 100 points (bonus); 3 (black): −100 points (penalty)

## Melds
A meld is 3 or more cards of the same rank. Each meld must contain at least 2 natural cards and no more than 3 wild cards (Jokers and 2s).

- **Clean book (red book):** 7 natural cards, no wilds — 500 point bonus
- **Dirty book (black book):** 7 cards with at least one wild — 300 point bonus

## Gameplay
On your turn: draw 2 cards from the stock (or take the discard pile under certain conditions), lay down or add to melds, then discard one card.

When your hand is empty, pick up your foot and continue playing.

## Going Out
To go out, a team must have at least one clean book and one dirty book, and one player must empty their foot. Going out earns a 100-point bonus.

## Scoring
Sum all meld card values plus book bonuses, minus any cards remaining in hand/foot and black 3 penalties.

## Winning
First team to reach 10,000 points across multiple rounds wins.
