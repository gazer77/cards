# Gin Rummy

Gin Rummy is a 2-player card game. The goal is to form your hand into sets and runs and "go out" before your opponent.

## Card Values
- Number cards (2–10): face value
- Jack, Queen, King: 10
- Ace: 1

## Melds
- **Set (Group):** 3 or 4 cards of the same rank (e.g., 7♠ 7♥ 7♦)
- **Run (Sequence):** 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., 4♣ 5♣ 6♣)

## Setup
Deal 10 cards to each player. Place remaining cards face-down as the stock. Flip the top card face-up to start the discard pile.

## Gameplay
On your turn, draw the top card from either the stock or the discard pile, then discard one card face-up.

## Going Out
- **Knock:** If your unmatched cards (deadwood) total 10 or fewer, you may knock. Lay down your melds; your opponent may lay off cards onto your melds.
- **Gin:** If all 10 cards form melds (0 deadwood), you "go Gin" for a bonus.
- **Undercut:** If your opponent knocks but you have equal or less deadwood, you undercut them for a bonus.

## Scoring
- **Knock:** Winner scores the difference in deadwood values
- **Gin:** +25 bonus points (opponent cannot lay off)
- **Undercut:** Defender scores difference + 25 bonus

## Winning
First player to reach 100 points wins the game.
