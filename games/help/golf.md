# Golf

Golf is a card game for 2–6 players. Like the sport, the goal is to finish with the lowest score.

## Setup
Deal 6 cards face-down to each player in a 2×3 grid. Players may not look at their cards. Each player flips 2 of their cards face-up. Remaining cards form the draw pile; flip one card to start the discard pile.

## Card Values
- 2: −2
- Ace: 1
- 3–10: face value
- Jack, Queen: 10
- King: 0
- Pair of matching cards in the same column: 0 (they cancel out)

## Gameplay
On your turn, draw the top card from either the stock or the discard pile. You may:
- Swap it with any card in your grid (face it up); or
- Discard it (if drawn from stock only), then flip one of your face-down cards face-up.

## Ending the Round
When a player has all 6 cards face-up, each other player takes one final turn. Then all cards are revealed and scored.

## Scoring
Sum the values of all 6 cards, applying column-pair cancellations. Record the score for the round.

## Winning
Play 9 rounds (holes). The player with the lowest total score wins.
