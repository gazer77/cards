# Euchre (3-Player)

Three-player Euchre uses the same 24-card deck and trump rules as standard Euchre but plays without fixed partnerships.

## Setup
Deal 5 cards to each player. One hand of 3 cards is set aside as a "kitty." The top card of the kitty is flipped to propose trump.

## Calling Trump
Same two-round process as 4-player Euchre. If a player orders up the flipped card, the dealer takes the entire kitty and discards 3 cards.

## Lone Play
The player who calls trump plays alone against the other two players, who cooperate as a temporary team.

## Scoring
- **Caller wins 3–4 tricks:** 1 point
- **Caller wins all 5 (march):** 3 points
- **Caller is euchred (fewer than 3 tricks):** Each opponent scores 2 points

## Winning
First player to reach 10 points wins.
